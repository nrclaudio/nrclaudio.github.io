spVIPES: Spatial Variational Inference for Perturbation Effects

I designed spVIPES, a model that disentangles biological variation from perturbation effects in spatial transcriptomics. The architecture combines variational inference with a novel optimal transport alignment to compare treated vs. control tissues at the single-spot level.
